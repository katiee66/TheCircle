Profile & settings

Your profile

The Circle is intended for adults aged 18+. This prototype checks the date entered but does not verify identity or age. A real service needs suitable age assurance.

Privacy & safety

Only invite people you know. Never share passwords, full bank details or one-time passcodes in group chat. Report concerns to your group admin and agree a clear dispute process.

User guide & terms

How to use The Circle

  1. Create an account and confirm you are at least 18.
  2. Create a group or request to join with a private invitation code.
  3. Invite up to 9 other people. The admin approves every member.
  4. Agree the contribution, due date, payout order and missed-payment rules together.
  5. Use the dashboard to record payment status and the chat to coordinate.
  6. Review the payout schedule and keep your own bank records.

Draft group terms

Draft for discussion only — not legal advice or legally approved terms.

  1. Membership: One active group per member in this prototype; maximum 10 approved adults per group.
  2. Contributions: Members agree the amount, currency and due date before the cycle starts.
  3. Payouts: The group records the recipient and payout order in advance. Changes require group agreement.
  4. Missed payments: Agree reminders, grace periods and a fair process before anyone joins.
  5. Leaving: Agree how outstanding contributions and future payouts are handled before the cycle begins.
  6. Privacy: Share only information needed to run the group. Do not post banking credentials or sensitive documents.
  7. Disputes: Try a documented group discussion first; seek independent advice where needed.

Important prototype limits

  • No real account creation, email or phone verification.
  • No live cross-device group chat or end-to-end encryption.
  • No bank connection, payment processing, custody or transfer of money.
  • Payment statuses are manually entered, not bank-verified.
  • Exchange rates are illustrative and not live.
  • Data is stored in this browser only and may be cleared.
