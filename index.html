How The Circle works

Get the group aligned before the first contribution is due.

1

Create or join

Start a circle or request to join using a private invitation. The group is designed for up to 10 approved members.

2

Agree the plan

Decide the contribution amount, group currency, due date, payout order and what happens if a payment is missed.

3

Track together

Use the shared dashboard and group conversation to keep everyone informed throughout the cycle.

Built around clarity

Trust starts with clear expectations.

Group saving depends on people understanding and agreeing the rules. The Circle puts those practical conversations up front.

  • Agree the rules before contributions begin.
  • Keep payment status visible to group members.
  • Use only invitations for people you trust.
  • Keep personal banking credentials private.

What The Circle is — and isn't

The current version is a concept prototype for organising a savings group. It does not collect, hold, transfer or verify money. Payment statuses are manually recorded, chat is local to the browser, and exchange rates are illustrative. A production service would need secure accounts, reliable infrastructure, appropriate legal review and payment integrations.
